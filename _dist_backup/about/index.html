About Us

About

Expert Power is Saudi Arabia's leading ISO 17025 accredited calibration laboratory, delivering precision calibration services since 2016. We combine technical expertise with exceptional service to exceed expectations.

Our Purpose

Mission

To provide accurate, reliable, and traceable calibration services that ensure the integrity of measurement processes across industries in Saudi Arabia. We are committed to supporting our clients' quality objectives through technical excellence and superior customer service.

Our Aspiration

Vision

To be the premier calibration and engineering solutions provider in the Middle East, recognized for technical excellence, innovation, and unwavering commitment to quality. We aim to set the benchmark for calibration services in the region.
